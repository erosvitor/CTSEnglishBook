Grammer - Questions in the past

Transform to question





Verbo To Be - Passado

Frases contendo verbos regulares

  • Passado: You worked a lot
  • Interrogativa: Did you work a lot?
  • Passado: She studied with you
  • Interrogativa: Did she study with you?

Frases contendo verbos irregulares:

  • Passado: You spoke with Mary
  • Interrogativa: Did you speak with Mary?
  • Passado: She went to school
  • Interrogativa: Did she go to school?
