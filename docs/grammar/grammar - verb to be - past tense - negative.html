Grammer - Verb To Be - Past Tense - Negative

Negate the verb To Be in the past





Verbo To Be - Passado
  • I was not (Eu não era/estava, não fui/estive)
  • You were not (Você não era/estava, Tu não eras/estavas)
  • He was not (Ele não era/estava)
  • She was not (Ela não era/estava)
  • It was not (Ela não era/estava)
  • We were not (Nós não erámos/estávamos)
  • You were not (Vós não éreis/estáveis)
  • They were not (Eles não eram/estavam)

Formas contraídas:

  • was not - wasn't
  • were not - weren't
